## 3. Anticipated Questions
 
| # | Question | Owner | Short answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is audio recorded or stored? | Briar Elliot | No. Audio is transcribed, turned into a constraint, and discarded. Only the constraint lasts for the trip. |
| 2 | What about passengers who did not agree to be recorded? | Dominic Rowland | A visible listening indicator shows when the mic is on. One tap mutes it, and the mic stops within 1 second. |
| 3 | Why send audio to OpenAI instead of processing it on the phone? | Briar Elliot | We use OpenAI under a no-retention setting. On-device transcription would keep audio off servers. We have not finalized this. |
| 4 | Can Veer change the route without asking? | Grady Algire | No. The route changes only after the driver says "okay." Silence or an unclear reply keeps the current route. |
| 5 | Is talking to Veer legal while driving? | Dominic Rowland | Ohio law (ORC 4511.204) allows voice use. Veer never asks the driver to tap the screen while moving. |
| 6 | Can businesses pay to be shown as stops? | Brady Cooper | No. Stops are ranked only by the driver's stated preference and detour cost. |
| 7 | How do you keep Google Maps costs and terms under control? | Brady Cooper | We use free credits and cache place data for no more than 30 days. Guidance terms check is still open. |
| 8 | What counts as rain? | Dominic Rowland | Not decided yet. The rain threshold is still open. |
| 9 | Will the app run up a big bill? | Aiden Ward | No. Usage alerts go out at 80% of each monthly cap. Paid calls are blocked at 100%. |
| 10 | What happens if the network drops during a trip? | Grady Algire | Veer keeps the active route and speaks a short prompt. |
| 11 | How well does speech recognition handle accents? | Briar Elliot | Not tested yet. Accent testing is planned for next semester. |
| 12 | What if the live demo fails? | Dominic Rowland | We play a pre-recorded screen capture. |
